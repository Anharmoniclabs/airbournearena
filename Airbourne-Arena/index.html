ARENA CORE

AIRBOURNE ARENA · 4 v 4 · VANGUARD WEST vs INFERNO EAST · FIRST TO 3

Four aircraft launch from the blue base in the west, four from the red base in the east. Between them, hanging at midfield, is a hardened Arena Core. Fly through it to pick it up, then run it to the ring at the enemy end to score. Shoot the carrier down and the case drops where they died, free for anyone. Wrecks are back in the air off their own base in 10 seconds.

The aircraft flies on a real velocity vector — thrust, lift, drag and gravity, with angle of attack driving the lift curve. Point the nose up and the jet keeps going the way it was going until the wing turns it. Pull too hard and the wing stalls. Trade height for speed.

Gunnery is solved, not guessed. The amber box marks the bandit the aircraft is tracking, the red circle is where they will be when your rounds arrive, and the pipper is where those rounds will actually be at that moment. Put the pipper on the circle — when the solution is good both go green. Rounds carry your own velocity, so a hard turn throws them wide.

On a phone the left thumb gets a floating stick, the right thumb gets FIRE, PASS, BOOST and ROLL. Works in whichever way you're holding it — no rotating required. ROLL snaps a barrel roll that corkscrews the flight path sideways and drags you out of somebody's gunsight.
